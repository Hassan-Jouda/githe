# Ghaith (غيث)
## Humanitarian Assistance & Charity Coordination Mobile Application

**Android Application | Kotlin | Humanitarian Technology | Social Impact | 2021**

### Project Overview

**Ghaith** is a humanitarian Android application developed in 2021 to bridge the gap between individuals in need, charitable organizations, and potential donors in the Gaza Strip.

The project was created with a mission: **to harness mobile technology to make humanitarian assistance more accessible, organized, transparent, and connected to the communities that need it most.**

Developed using **Kotlin** for the Android platform, Ghaith emerged from a collaborative student initiative carried out in cooperation with the **Palestinian Ministry of Social Affairs and several local charitable associations and community organizations**.

The project explored how digital technology could address persistent humanitarian challenges by improving access to information about charitable services, facilitating communication between stakeholders, and helping connect vulnerable individuals and families with relevant assistance providers.

### The Problem

Humanitarian organizations provide essential assistance to vulnerable communities, but accessing this support can be challenging.

Individuals and families may struggle to identify appropriate organizations, locate available services, or determine where to request help. Meanwhile, donors and charitable institutions need effective ways to connect resources with community needs.

Ghaith was developed in response to these challenges, with a focus on using accessible mobile technology to reduce information gaps and support better coordination between humanitarian stakeholders.

### Our Solution

Ghaith was designed as a mobile platform connecting three key groups:

- **Beneficiaries:** Individuals and families seeking humanitarian assistance and information about available charitable services.
- **Charitable Organizations:** Local associations and humanitarian institutions providing community support.
- **Donors:** Individuals interested in identifying charitable initiatives and supporting people in need.

The application's central concept was to bring these groups together through a more accessible and structured digital environment.

### Key Project Features and Objectives

**1. Charitable Organization Directory**

Provide an accessible directory of charitable organizations, helping users identify organizations operating in their communities and understand where assistance may be available.

**2. Location-Based Assistance Discovery**

Help users identify the geographic locations of relevant charitable institutions, making it easier to find nearby organizations and access their services.

**3. Humanitarian Information Accessibility**

Improve access to information about charitable activities, available services, and potential sources of assistance.

**4. Donor–Beneficiary Connectivity**

Explore digital approaches for connecting potential donors, charitable organizations, and vulnerable populations to support more effective humanitarian outreach.

**5. Community-Centered Mobile Experience**

Design an Android application that emphasizes simplicity, accessibility, and practical usability for individuals with different levels of technical experience.

**6. Coordination with Local Stakeholders**

Incorporate insights from cooperation with the Palestinian Ministry of Social Affairs and local associations to better understand community needs and the practical challenges faced by humanitarian service providers.
